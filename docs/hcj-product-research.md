Research date: 2026-08-08. These URLs were read only to compare buyer questions, parameter categories and information architecture. No competitor copy, images, company information, claims or customers were used in public HCJ content.

## Product-by-product scope

The three rod-family references above were used as architecture checks for Hard Chrome Plated Rod, CK45 Chrome Plated Rod, 20MnV6 Chrome Plated Rod, Induction Hardened Chrome Rod, Hollow Chrome Plated Rod and Finished Piston Rod. The three tube-family references above were used for Honed Tube, ST52 Honed Tube, CK45 Honed Tube and Skived and Roller Burnished Tube. For each HCJ page, the retained fields are restricted to facts already supported by the HCJ repository or approved instructions; unverified numbers and grades were omitted.

## Buyer questions retained in HCJ's original wording

- Which material designation, dimensions, tolerance and finished condition are on the approved drawing?
- Is the request for a rod, a finished piston rod, a honed tube or an SRB tube?
- Which end machining, OD/ID, surface or bore condition must be reviewed?
- Which inspection evidence and packing protection are required for the order?
- Which hydraulic-cylinder application explains the component requirement?

The research did not establish any HCJ product performance values, certificate status, delivery terms, inventory, pricing, salt-spray result or inspection standard. Those items remain absent from public pages until independently approved.
